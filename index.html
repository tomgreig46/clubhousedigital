    FAQ

    Fair questions.

    What do I pay after the build?

    Nothing to us. Your hosting and domain are paid by you, directly to the provider, in your club's name. We charge no ongoing agency subscription.

    Do you build the booking or payment system for golf courses?

    No. Your course already has a system, and we build the website around it. Our job is how it looks and how easy it is to reach: prominent booking buttons that open your own system.

    Who owns the website?

    You do. You receive the files, and your domain and hosting sit in your own account.

    What if we want changes later?

    Any web developer can edit the files, including us if you ask. Content sits in one clear place, so simple updates are quick.

    Why is it cheaper than an agency?

    We use AI-assisted design and a repeatable build, and skip the long agency process. You get a fixed price and a tight scope.

    Will the contact form on my site work?

    Yes, on your delivered site. It needs a small form handler on your hosting, which we set up and test on your account before handover.
